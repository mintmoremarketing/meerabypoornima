Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Last Updated: 25 August 2026

Meera by Poornima Sharma (“Meera,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) values the trust you place in us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, get in touch about a commission, or purchase a piece from us — and how we use, share, and protect that information.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that helps us serve you well. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number when you fill in our contact form or email us directly.
  • Order and appointment details — measurements, event dates, delivery addresses, and design preferences shared during a commission or showroom visit.
  • Photos and testimonials — imagery and words you choose to share with us, which we may feature only with your written consent.
  • Website usage data — anonymous browser type, device, referring page, and pages viewed, gathered through standard analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, and coordinate fittings or deliveries.
  • To send you updates about your order or booked appointment.
  • To improve the design, safety, and usability of our website.
  • To share, with your permission only, photographs and reviews on our website and social channels.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or advertising profiling.

Sharing With Third Parties

Your information is handled with care and shared only where necessary to fulfil your order or run this website:

  • Our couriers and logistics partners, for delivery.
  • Our payment processor, for authorised transactions — we never see or store your full card details.
  • Our website and email service providers, under confidentiality agreements.
  • Regulatory or legal authorities, where we are required by Australian law to do so.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies — brief pieces of data stored by your browser — to remember your preferences and to help us understand which pages resonate most. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

Photography & Client Imagery

Every wedding and pre-wedding shoot featured on this site is used with the client’s permission. If you have appeared in our imagery and would like it removed, please email us and we will action your request promptly.

How We Protect Your Data

We use industry-standard security practices — encrypted connections, restricted access, and secure hosting — to safeguard the information you share with us. No online transmission is ever 100% secure, so please do not send sensitive details (such as full card numbers) over email.

Your Rights

You can, at any time:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to any legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Unsubscribe from any non-transactional communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email meerabypoornimasharma@gmail.com.

Children

This website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The date at the top of this page will always show when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us at meerabypoornimasharma@gmail.com — we read every message.
