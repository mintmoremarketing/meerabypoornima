Kersha & Thinesh

Kersha Thinesh Pre-wedding Shoot

Kersha was my first Bride who wanted me to design her a western style gown for her pre-wedding shoot. She trusted me to deliver her vision just the way she had imagined.

I designed her gown in Pure Crepe Silk, got the digital stripes done on them & to give a full flow & body I added Cancan underneath with Shantoon Silk lining.

When she received the dress she was so happy, excited and very double minded whether to make it short or leave it long… well looks like she took the best option not only with the gown but also with her partner in crime Thinesh.

I can’t wait to share their Wedding photos showcasing their Wedding outfits which we envisioned aesthetically to suit their Mudgee Country style wedding.

Akshara & Krish

Akshara & Krish Pre-Wedding Shoot

When Akshara & Krish video called me to give me their Engagement news I was over the moon for them.

Akshara wanted to wear a traditional Saree for her Pre Wedding Shoot so I designed a gorgeous deep Purple Banarasi Pure Silk Saree with Meenakari Antique Gold Boutis & Taffetta Silk Gold Gotta high neck / front V style deep Blouse.

I paired the outfit with Silver based Gold antique Kundan choker & earrings set.

She looked super gorgeous in her final look. Can’t wait for her wedding festivities.
